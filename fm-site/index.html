Energy resilience · 12 Nov 2026

AI growth is reshaping backup-power planning

Facilities Dive reports that the growth of data centres is prompting closer examination of whether diesel generators should remain the default backup option. Battery energy storage is also part of the resilience discussion. FM takeaway: assess reliability, safety, emissions, lifecycle cost and site-specific load requirements before selecting backup systems.

Public industry report
Electrification · 12 Nov 2026

Businesses call for faster electrification

Facilities Dive reports that more than 180 companies have renewed calls to accelerate electrification. For facility teams, this reinforces the value of tracking building energy use, equipment efficiency, electrical capacity and practical transition plans.

Public industry report
Cleaning & hygiene · November 2026

Industry attention on indoor air quality and water efficiency

CleanLink’s November industry roundup highlights a national survey on indoor-air-quality knowledge gaps and cleaning-sector attention to water-efficiency measures. FM takeaway: combine cleaning quality checks with ventilation awareness and measurable water-use practices.
